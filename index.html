The moments

Life is not derailed by the things we planned for.

The real risk is the stuff we never saw coming. And yet the same handful of ordinary moments keep asking for the same information, and it keeps getting written on whatever was nearby.

Back to school

New teachers, new forms, new pickup rules. Every August asks you to write it all down again.

A new babysitter

You type the same novel into Messages every time. Allergies, bedtime, the address for the delivery apps.

Your weekend away

The trip you earned. And the 11pm "where is the thermometer" text you did not.

Grandparents on duty

They raised you just fine. They still do not know the inhaler lives in the junk drawer.

The ER, at 2am

Insurance ID, med doses, allergy history. The intake clipboard does not wait for you to check your email.

The one you can't predict

Emergencies do not check your calendar first. The plan you make on a calm Tuesday is the one that shows up.

How it works

From your head to the fridge in three steps

Fill in the blanks

A guided Emergency Card for your family: doctors, allergies, meds, backup adults, house rules. Every section you finish boosts your score. Most families who complete it land above 85.

3

Stick it on the fridge

Print one Letter-size sheet, one row per kid, each with its own QR code. Paper holds the basics a sitter needs at a glance. The code carries everything else, and it stays current when you update online.

Two layers

Paper for the glance. QR for the full picture.

A babysitter at the fridge and a nurse at intake need different depths of the same truth. One sheet serves both.

On the paper

Readable from four feet away, holding a crying toddler.

  • 911 and Poison Control, right at the top
  • Your home address, spoken aloud to dispatchers
  • Both parents' numbers, plus the backup adults
  • Each kid's allergies, flagged so they cannot be missed
  • The pediatrician's name and number
  • A QR code per kid, for everything below

Behind the QR

One scan opens that child's full, current page.

  • Allergy reactions, and exactly what to do
  • Medications with doses and where they live
  • Insurance carrier and member ID for intake
  • The full care team: doctor, dentist, preferred hospital
  • The approved pickup list
  • The quirks only you know: bedtime, comfort items, the dog

Update online any time. The printed codes keep pointing at the current version, so the paper almost never goes stale.
