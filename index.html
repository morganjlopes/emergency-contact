The risk nobody plans for

The hard days are rarely the ones you saw coming.

You plan for what you can picture. What actually goes wrong is the thing nobody wrote down, on the day the person who knows it cannot be reached.

You are the only copy

The dose, the doctor, the allergy, the pickup rules. One person carries all of it, and that person is usually Mom.

You are not there when it matters

That is what makes it an emergency. Someone else has your kids and needs an answer before they can reach you.

It was never one document

It is forty small facts spread across your phone, the school portal, a text thread, and your memory.

After the kids

Start with your kids. They are the easiest yes.

Your partner

Half of this lives with only one of you. A shared card means either parent can answer any question, and so can anyone helping out.

Yourself

You are the one person you never write down. If it were you in the ambulance, your family would be guessing at your medicines and your doctor.

Your parents

The conversation you keep meaning to have gets much easier when you can open with the one you already made for your own kids.
